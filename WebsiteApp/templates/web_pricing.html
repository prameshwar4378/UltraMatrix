Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for schools comparing plans, billing and setup support.

Need help choosing? Start with Professional if you need AI, Excel exports and proxy handling. Starter is best for basic timetable management. Start Free Trial
Can I start with a free trial?

Yes. You can start a trial, add school data, test timetable creation and decide which plan fits your institute before continuing.

Can I upgrade or change my plan later?

Yes. You can begin with Starter and move to Professional when you need AI conflict solving, Excel exports or proxy adjustment tools.

Which plan includes AI timetable scheduling?

Professional includes AI scheduling features for most schools. Enterprise is better for custom rules, multi-school setups and advanced AI workflows.

Do you support PDF and Excel exports?

Starter includes PDF export. Professional includes both PDF and Excel exports, while Enterprise can support custom export requirements.

Is onboarding support included?

Professional includes priority support. Enterprise includes dedicated onboarding for schools that need guided setup, permissions and custom workflows.

Can one account manage multiple timetable versions?

Yes. Starter supports limited active drafts, while Professional and Enterprise are designed for unlimited planning versions and more advanced review flows.
